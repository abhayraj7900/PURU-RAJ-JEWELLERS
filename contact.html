Shipping & returns

Delivery

Orders are usually dispatched within 2–3 working days. Tracking details appear in My Account when the shipment is booked.

Shipping fee

Every jewellery order includes insured shipping. The final fee is shown securely before payment.

Returns

Contact us within 3 days of delivery for damaged or incorrect items. Items must remain unused and in their original packaging.

Need an answer?

Frequently asked questions

How do I place an order?

Add products to your bag, sign in, enter your delivery details and choose online payment or cash on delivery.

Can I pay with UPI?

Yes. Secure online checkout supports UPI, cards and net banking through Razorpay. Cash on delivery is available for eligible locations.

Where is my invoice?

Every confirmed order gets an invoice number. Open My Account to view, print or save the invoice.

How do I track delivery?

Your shipment and tracking details appear with the order in My Account after the courier booking is created.
